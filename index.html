Frequently Asked Q's

For guests within driving distance of the venue, there's free on-site parking that's about a five-minute walk from the venue.

PDX is closest to the wedding venue. Hood River is about an hour from PDX. Rental cars are a great option or if you need transport please contact us or our families. Please keep in mind that Hood River itself is a small town with limited public transit and ride hailing options.

We aren't allowing plus-ones at our wedding, as we'd like to keep the guests list just to those who know us well. The name of every guest invited to attend is clearly listed on your invitation and no additional guests can be accommodated.

We love your children, but we can't accommodate guests under 10 years old on our wedding day. However, we will have child care available on the property for anyone who has kids. Please let us know if you are bringing your kids, so we can make sure they are taken care of properly!

We'll be serving hors d'oeuvres at cocktail hour, followed by a sit-down dinner. All food served will be nut and sesame free. We will also have vegan and gluten-free option available. Later in the night we will serve dessert and after-party snacks for those attending the late-night festivities. If you have additional restrictions that go beyond what we've listed here, please let us know in the designated section of your RSVP. We want you to feel comfortable and well fed!

Our ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception will all be outside in “the Field.” For our out of town guests Hood River summer is hot and dry during the day and cooler at night. Plan on it being warm, verging on hot, sunny, and more than slightly windy. There will be shade and cover but dress accordingly.
